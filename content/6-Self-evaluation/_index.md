### Strengths

* **Solid technical foundation:** Strong grasp of the AWS Well-Architected Framework, serverless services (Lambda, API Gateway, Step Functions, S3, DynamoDB), and security best practices (IAM, Cognito, Secrets Manager).
* **Strong self-learning ability:** Proactively reads official AWS docs, completes courses on AWS Skill Builder, and immediately applies new knowledge to real projects.
* **Proactive and responsible:** Consistently meets deadlines and is willing to support teammates when needed.
* **Systems thinking:** Evaluates solutions from multiple angles — cost, performance, security, and scalability — before implementation.
* **Curious and growth-oriented:** Actively participates in workshops, blog challenges, and AWS Study Group activities to share knowledge with the community.

### Areas for Improvement

* **Strengthen personal discipline:** Maintain a more stable working schedule and avoid letting tasks pile up at the last minute.
* **Sharpen problem-solving thinking:** Practice root-cause analysis and evaluate multiple solution paths before committing to an approach.
* **Improve advanced communication skills:** Become more confident presenting solutions to larger audiences and practice writing technical documentation in English.
* **Deepen specialized knowledge:** Explore CloudWatch tracing, AWS X-Ray, cost optimization, and advanced distributed architecture patterns (event-driven, saga, CQRS) for larger-scale projects.

### Development Roadmap

* Pursue the **AWS Solutions Architect – Associate** and **AWS Developer – Associate** certifications within the next year.
* Contribute more actively to open-source projects and production-grade serverless projects to accumulate real-world experience.
* Publish in-depth technical articles on personal blog and AWS Study Group to share knowledge with the community.
