# Summary Report: “AWS COMMUNITY DAY”

### Event Objectives

- Connect the community of technology and AWS enthusiasts (First Cloud Journey).
- Share effective learning methods and techniques to "hack" the brain and maintain motivation.
- Answer career-orientation questions, especially for 3rd and 4th year students.
- Provide a space to discuss work mindset in the AI era.

### Speakers

- **1. Huynh Hoang Long** - Addicted to learning Like You're Addicted to Social Media
- **2. Nguyen Tuan Thinh** - Automated Prompt Engineering: Enhancing LLM Output Quality
- **3. Mr. Khang** - AI-Ready Freshers
- **4. Ms. Thao** - BMAD METHOD

### Key Highlights

- **Hack your brain for learning:**
	- Understand the role of *dopamine* to turn studying into an enjoyable, game-like activity.
	- Techniques to break down goals (e.g., splitting a running distance or an AWS service into smaller tasks) to avoid overwhelm.
	- Use the fear of "breaking the chain" (as seen with *TikTok* or *Duolingo*) to maintain daily learning habits.

- **Mindset in the AI era:**
	- AI can double productivity but cannot replace core foundational thinking.
	- A warning against abusing AI for assignments without understanding the underlying concepts (outsourcing thinking is dangerous).
	- Emphasize the importance of deep foundational understanding rather than surface-level familiarity with technologies.

### Learnings

- **Self-management skills:**
	- How to set up a study environment (desk decor, creating a focused workspace).
	- The 2-minute rule: if a task takes less than 2 minutes, do it immediately.

- **Career orientation:**
	- The 3-circle formula for work: Passion, Duty (what must be done), and Benefits received.
	- How to evaluate a job by more than salary — consider experience, exposure, and network.

- **Engineer mindset:**
	- The importance of integrity and long-term thinking in professional work.
	- Handling edge cases to produce higher-quality products.

### Application to Work

- Use AI as a brainstorming "assistant" but always verify and control output quality.
- Focus on foundations rather than chasing every new technology trend.
- Build team habits and contribute knowledge to the community to increase personal value.

### Event Experience

- An open atmosphere encouraging young attendees to ask questions and critique directly.
- Practical learning through everyday examples rather than dry theory.
- Opportunities to connect with like-minded people in the *First Cloud Journey* community.
