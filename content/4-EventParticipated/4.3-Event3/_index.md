# Summary Report: “AWS COMMUNITY DAY”

### Event Objectives

- Learn by doing: turn cloud and AI theory into practical skills through project building.
- Build a portfolio/experience: create real projects and sample prototypes for CV or personal GitHub.
- Promote the AI community (Agentic AI Build Week): gather thousands of AI builders to create hundreds of projects focused on Agentic AI.
- Connect and collaborate: create an environment for tech enthusiasts (especially students and Gen Z) to connect with experts and major partners like AWS and NVIDIA.

### Speakers

- **1. Nghia Tran** - Tu Vi Đai Viet
- **2. Nguyen Thi Quynh Nhu** - Why We Always Need Confidence
- **3. Khac Uy Pham** - The Iceberg of Procrastination
- **4. Huynh Thai Linh** - Level Up Your AWS Skills with Cloud Quest and Floci
- **5. The Ballers team** - Hackathon

### Key Highlights

#### Practical AI projects

- Introduced products such as Tử Vi Đại Việt (AI-based destiny interpretation) and SynthHunter (AI voice authentication system for fraud prevention).

#### Cloud infrastructure solutions

- Presented how to improve AWS skills through Cloud Quest.
- Compared performance between Floci (an internal AWS simulation environment) and LocalStack.

#### Personal development and soft skills

- Talks on the importance of confidence in career development.
- In-depth analysis of procrastination psychology (The Iceberg of Procrastination).

#### Hackathon experience

- Shared a 24–48 hour journey that was intense and exciting, solving real-world problems and creating MVP products.

### Learnings

- **Problem-solving skills**: The best ideas often come from solving real-life pain points.
- **The importance of perseverance**: Challenges and failures are an essential part of the creative process; continuous experimentation is needed to achieve the best outcome.
- **Work psychology**: Understand that procrastination often stems from fear (fear of failure, fear of judgment), not merely laziness.
- **Effective use of resources**: Know how to maximize available tools and adapt flexibly, combining ideas to overcome difficulties.

### Application to Work

- **Practical learning roadmap**: Apply a three-phase process: Thinking & Architecture (AWS Cloud Quest) -> Coding & rapid testing (Floci) -> Real deployment (Real AWS).
- **Software project management**: Be aware of the hidden cost of seemingly simple features, which can cause mistakes, rework, and team burnout.
- **DevOps process optimization**: Identify surface symptoms such as delays or production bugs to find root causes like lack of ownership or poor communication between teams.
- **Building professional confidence**: Use confidence as a tool to enhance communication, collaboration, and effective connection in the workplace.
