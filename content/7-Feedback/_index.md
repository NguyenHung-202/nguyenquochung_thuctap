> You are welcome to freely share your personal opinions about your experience in the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program. Your feedback will help the FCAJ team improve the program across the following aspects:

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is friendly, open, and supportive. FCAJ members are always ready to help when I run into technical blockers, even outside official hours. The workspace is organized and comfortable, which helps me focus deeply on the work. A small note: adding more team-bonding activities would help interns connect with one another better.

**2. Support from Mentor / Admin**  
Mentors provide detailed guidance, explain architectural concepts clearly, and consistently encourage me to research first before asking for help. The admin team handles logistics and documentation smoothly, creating optimal conditions for me to work efficiently. I especially value the mentor's "let the intern debug first" style — it builds real self-learning ability.

**3. Relevance to My Academic Major**  
The assigned tasks align well with the software engineering foundation I learned at university, while also expanding my view into modern cloud-native domains. Moving from theoretical design to actually building production-grade cloud infrastructure helped me reinforce theory and absorb real industry practice.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
I gained hands-on practice in Agile project management, Git collaboration standards in an enterprise context, and structured technical communication. The real-world experience shared by mentors gave me sharper, data-driven insight to plan my long-term career as a Full-Stack Cloud Engineer.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The culture is positive: people respect each other, tackle technical problems seriously, yet keep the atmosphere welcoming. During tight project sprints, the whole team naturally supported one another regardless of seniority. That made me feel like a real member of the engineering team — not just an intern.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The program provides a motivating internship allowance and flexible working hours, which helped me balance university obligations. Exclusive access to internal training sessions and mentor-led workshops is a standout benefit of the program.

### Additional Questions

* **What I found most satisfying during the internship:**  
  Moving from paper-level design to actually building the **AI-powered English Essay Scoring System** that runs on production. Watching the state machine process payloads, trigger the Gemini API, and persist results gave me huge practical confidence.

* **What the company could improve for future interns:**  
  Onboarding documentation for advanced topics (e.g. hybrid subnet, custom IAM condition keys) could go deeper. A short "common pitfalls cheat-sheet" from day one would dramatically accelerate debugging for new interns.

* **Would I recommend this internship to friends? Why:**  
  Absolutely. Unlike traditional internships that limit students to documentation tasks, FCAJ pushes interns into real technical challenges — serverless, advanced IAM, GenAI integrations — under expert mentorship.

### Suggestions & Expectations

* **Suggestion to improve the experience:**  
  Organize a **Cross-Team Architecture Review** mid-program, where different project teams present design choices, cost calculations, and structural mistakes they hit. That would unlock huge peer-to-peer learning.

* **Would I like to continue with this program in the future:**  
  Yes. I want to stay connected with FCAJ, possibly as an alumni or a mentor for future cohorts.

* **Any other comments:**  
  Thank you to all mentors, admins, and teammates for making this journey a solid stepping stone into a professional enterprise engineering environment.
